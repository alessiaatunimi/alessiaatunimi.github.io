I study how networks evolve and what they can tell us about human behaviour. My work brings together **network science, machine learning, and computational social science**, with applications ranging from decentralised social platforms to climate activism.

I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Milan, awarded with honours and the Doctor Europaeus distinction. After a postdoctoral position at the IT University of Copenhagen, I returned to Milan, where I am a research fellow.
