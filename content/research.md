## Temporal and multilayer networks

How do networks grow, and which patterns of change are meaningful? My research develops and applies **graph evolution rules** to study dynamic networks at the local and community levels. This includes statistically significant temporal patterns, time-ordered triadic closure, and representations of temporal ego-networks.

Applications include blockchain-based networks, NFT markets, cashless payments, and customer segmentation.

## Computational social science

I study how people participate in online platforms: how they migrate between communities, cooperate, vote, and engage in public discourse. My work on **climate communication** examines the landscape of climate change content on TikTok and the relationship between news coverage and online discussion about Greta Thunberg.

[Publications in computational social science →](/publications/?area=social)

## Machine learning on graphs

I combine network modelling with machine learning to analyse and predict behaviour, including user migration in blockchain socioeconomic networks. Related collaborations explore anomaly detection in networks and the analysis of Web3 platforms.
