I am a researcher at the **University of Milan**, working in machine learning, network science, and computational social science. I am interested in how social and financial networks evolve, and what their patterns reveal about human behaviour.

Teaching is also an important part of my work. I enjoy helping students develop the tools to ask their own questions of data.

## Background

I earned my **PhD in Computer Science at the University of Milan** in 2024, with honours and the Doctor Europaeus distinction. My thesis, *A Framework for Network Evolution*, explored how to understand change in complex networks.

Before returning to Milan in 2026, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the **NERDS Lab, IT University of Copenhagen**, working with Luca Maria Aiello on climate activism and user behaviour on social media. My research has also taken me to CIRAD in Montpellier and the University of Chicago, and I collaborate with the **HEAL Lab at Bocconi University**.

## Academic community

I am an **Associate Editor of Applied Network Science** and lead guest editor of its *Evolution of Networks* special issue.

I enjoy building spaces where researchers can exchange ideas. I am an executive member of **NETPLACE** and its representative on the **NetSci Colloquium Committee**, co-founded the **MUSEMI** seminar series in Milan, and contribute to **CORE**, a community initiative in complexity and network science.
